## Brand notes

- **Primary colours**: White text/logo on dark background
- **Format**: The header SVG is a wordmark (combination of K symbol + "kappa" text + "lab" text); the Apple touch icon is the preferred brandmark for small-screen use
- The brandmark has transparent background and is suitable for generic, light, and dark metadata variants
- No dedicated brand kit or press kit page was found; the icon was sourced from the official Webflow-hosted website asset
