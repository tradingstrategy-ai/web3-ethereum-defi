# Ryllo curator logo

The [official Ryllo website](https://ryllo.co/) links the orange brand mark
as its favicon and serves it from its own brand assets directory.

- Source: [Ryllo orange brand mark](https://ryllo.co/assets/brand/ryllo-mark-orange.png)
- Original: `ryllo.generic.png`, 516 × 516 pixels, RGBA PNG, 3,542 bytes.
- Downloaded: 2026-10-22.

The original is square and transparent. The repository's
`scripts/logos/post-process-logo.py` trims transparent margins, preserves the
orange brand colour and produces a 256 × 256 transparent PNG. The same
processed mark is used for `light.png` and `dark.png`, as it remains visible
on both backgrounds and requires no recolouring.
