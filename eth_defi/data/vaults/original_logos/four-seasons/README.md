# Four Seasons logo source

`four-seasons.generic.svg` is the Four Seasons partner logo displayed by the
official Asseto product API. It is a 504 x 80 wordmark, not a compact
brandmark.

Source: <https://static.asseto.finance/asseto/2026-02-25/dgnvdq14496mckrjjs.svg>

Downloaded on 2026-07-16.

## Brandmark investigation

Asseto identifies this partner as Four Seasons Investment Management Limited,
the investment manager of CMF FS Asia Equity Opportunity SP. The public
metadata has no website, social account, or brand-kit link for this entity:
<https://vault-protocol-metadata.tradingstrategy.ai/curator-metadata/index.json>.
The related DL Holdings disclosures identify the legal entity but publish no
Four Seasons icon: <https://www.dl-holdings.com/download/IR/Announcements%20and%20Notices/en/EW01709-ann_34.pdf>.

No authoritative icon-only Four Seasons Investment Management asset could be
verified. The Four Seasons Hotels and Resorts tree mark and unrelated Four
Seasons investment-adviser logos were not substituted because they represent
different organisations. We therefore did not derive an artificial lettermark
from the wordmark and did not generate `light.png`/`dark.png`; the existing
`generic.png` remains the source wordmark pending an official compact asset.
