## Notes

- No dedicated brand kit or press page was found on the website
- The wordmark SVG contains the orange "T" geometric brand mark on the left and full company name in white on the right; it is not used for the standard vault logo because compact brandmarks are preferred.
- The SVG uses a transparent background with `#E06339` (orange) for the brand mark and `#F2F2F2` (near-white) for the text
- The favicon has transparent pixels and is the source for the processed `generic.png`, `light.png`, and `dark.png` outputs.
- The brand mark is designed for dark backgrounds; the orange mark remains visible on both themes.
