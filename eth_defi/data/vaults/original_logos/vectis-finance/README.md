# Vectis Finance logo

Downloaded on 9 November 2026 from the [official homepage](https://www.vectis.finance/).
The [frameless SVG brand mark](https://www.vectis.finance/icons/vectis.svg) was
selected for publication. No brand kit or GitHub branding link was present there.

The original `vectis-finance.generic.svg` has a 46 × 42 view box, transparent
background and turquoise symbol. The repository's
`scripts/logos/post-process-logo.py` produced the transparent 256 × 256 RGBA
`formatted_logos/vectis-finance/generic.png`. No separate theme variants were
available in the inspected homepage logo assets. The homepage's
[framed variant](https://www.vectis.finance/icons/vectis-icon-with-bg.svg) is
preserved as `vectis-finance.framed.svg` (136 × 109); its translucent card and
radial glow make it less suitable for small icons.
