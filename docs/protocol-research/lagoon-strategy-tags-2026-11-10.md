## Decision boundaries and remaining gaps

- Genesis/Fundatio describes low-leverage Hyperliquid trading without identifying spot versus perpetual instruments. It remains unmapped; the venue alone does not justify a perpetual-futures tag.
- Denomination-asset beta alone is not tagged as directional trading: Ammalgam WETH and DAMM Optimism retain their income/market-making tags without an additional directional tag. Active rotation, outright index/perpetual positions and discretionary asset selection still qualify.
- Gami xBTCY cbBTC receives liquidity_provider only: specific AMM venues named by the sister WBTC deployment are not assumed for it.
- Vetro master/feeder descriptions identify the structure and assets, but do not identify the underlying yield mechanisms. They remain unmapped rather than inheriting unrelated Gami vault strategies.
- Hub Capital crvUSD provides an Aave-relative return target without identifying the mechanism. It remains unmapped. The pre-existing Hub USDC classification is retained with its existing documented sources, not copied to crvUSD.
- BOLD forks and TAC points-only pre-deposit vaults do not identify enough mechanics. A benchmark, token name, curator name, broad platform category or generic yield claim is not used to infer lending, RWA, perpetual, or leveraged exposure.
- Internal test descriptions are retained as inventory evidence, not treated as production strategies. A missing API record does not establish whether a deployment is active or closed.
- UEB3 supports only `multistrategy`; Treasury yields are its benchmark rather than documented portfolio holdings. MuDigital/Tulipa supports lending and liquidity from its exact API description; its token names alone are not used to add RWA subtypes.
- The current Ammalgam WETH mandate explicitly targets approximately 100% ETH delta, so `delta_neutral` is removed. Both Ammalgam descriptions discuss impermanent loss rather than an actual options allocation, so `options` is removed.
- SP500 and SpaceX synthetic portfolios target roughly 1× overall exposure. Account-level leverage/margin settings do not justify `directional_leverage`. SP500 also explicitly allocates idle capital to lending.
- The [official VOLTA90 migration announcement](https://volta90.trade/blog/volta90-ethereum-migration) names both contracts and the unchanged Hyperliquid book. Arbitrum is classified historically and remains closed; this change does not splice price histories or alter listing eligibility. Its [current operator description](https://volta90.trade/) distinguishes human position selection from automatic execution and risk control.
- The [Regime Vault strategy](https://regimevault.com/strategy) corroborates model-based Bitcoin sizing and Aave idle-capital lending. Human approval does not erase the programmed model; no leverage or trend-following claim is inferred.
- Existing comments for GUSDC and MSA Dynamique now explain the strategy evidence instead of conflating strategy tags with curator attribution. No curator assignment changes here.
