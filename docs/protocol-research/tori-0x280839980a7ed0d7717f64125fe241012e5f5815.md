## Attribution and strategy

Native strUSD staking accepts trUSD and distributes rewards through its share
exchange rate. Tori operates the product and its
[privileged roles](https://docs.tori.finance/security/roles). No separate
independent curator is established for this native contract.

The [ecosystem vault documentation](https://docs.tori.finance/resources/pre-deposit)
and [RockawayX's product disclosure](https://rockawayx.com/insights/rockawayx-tori-ecosystem-vault-investment-thesis-risk-disclosure)
identify a separate Upshift vault issuing etrUSD, at
`0xcd69123b3fbbfc666e1f6a501da27b564c00de54`. RockawayX curates that product.
Its fee schedule, allocations and curator identity must not be copied to the
native staking vault.

Tori's [strategy overview](https://docs.tori.finance/strategy/overview) describes
short-duration money-market instruments, spot/futures arbitrage and calendar
spreads under a market-neutral mandate. The maintained native staking tags are
`multistrategy`, `delta_neutral`, `arbitrage` and `carry_trade`. These sources
do not establish perpetual-futures or funding-rate-arbitrage exposure.

## Fees, withdrawals and risks

The staking terms state that staking, unstaking and the staked token amount
have no separate charge. Tori retains an unspecified share of gross trading
income before distributing net rewards. Management, deposit and withdrawal
fee values are therefore zero; the numerical performance fee and fee mode
remain unknown. The separate
[minting and redemption service](https://docs.tori.finance/resources/institutional)
lists a 0.1% charge per operation as at the review date.

The documented cooldown is seven days, followed by a claim for trUSD. The
operator can change the delay, and a zero cooldown permits direct redemption.
The adapter reads the configured value instead of relying on a fixed default.
Public transaction-manager support is unavailable in this catalogue integration.

The [backing description](https://docs.tori.finance/solution/backing) includes
externally held positions. The [risk disclosures](https://docs.tori.finance/resources/risks)
cover trading, counterparty, custody and contract risks. The implementation
permits loss reporting; appreciation is not guaranteed. No technical risk
rating has been assigned in this change.

## Onchain and integration checks

Reads using the supplied Ethereum RPC configuration returned the canonical
trUSD asset, 18 decimals and a 604,800-second cooldown at block 25,598,869 and
at current block 26,298,713. At the fixed block, total assets were
`25191902324647899163613529` raw trUSD and total supply was
`25014022102434023833995912` raw strUSD.

Reading the proxy's EIP-1967 implementation slot on the review date returned
`0x6561272e3EbC9f2e07cDB12B7c474Db9132b977a`. Explorer HTTP access was blocked
and Sourcify's legacy metadata endpoints did not contain this implementation;
the reviewed contract source is Tori's published repository. No new ABI is
needed: the adapter uses the shared ERC-4626 interface and one canonical
`cooldownDuration()` selector.

Manual local integration result, 29 October 2026: both tests passed using the
user-supplied Ethereum mainnet providers, with credentials redacted. This was
a local run, not CI:

```shell
source .local-test.env && timeout 180s poetry run pytest tests/erc_4626/vault_protocol/test_tori.py -q --tb=short --log-cli-level=info
```

The module covers a shared fixed-block Anvil fork and a direct current-state
provider read. Neither test sends a transaction. Migration and publication
instructions are in `docs/source/vaults/tori/deployment.rst`; this change has
not been applied to production or published.
