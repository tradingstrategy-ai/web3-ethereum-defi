## Corrected recovery policy

The operator requires the **price-row timestamp as the observation clock when
no independent permission clock is available**. Recovered backup flags therefore
supply historical permission with `legacy_price_timestamp` provenance. Open on
15–17 September is usable under this approximation. The first retained disabled
flag has price timestamp **21 September 11:48:00.756**, with original write time
11:48:02.468780. At midnight the corrected rule yields **Open through 21 September**
and **Closed from 22 September**. This is a retained flag with an inferred clock,
not proof of the exact venue transition time.

Genuine receipts, including explicit unknown responses, take precedence. No
backup flags means Unknown. Capacity freshness is never inferred. There are
**zero independently clocked API receipts** for DOEZOE in these recovered files;
price-row counts and repeated flags are not independent measurements. Flags in
raw exports may already have been carried forwards, so their price-clock age is
an approximation accepted by the recovery policy.

## Consumer behaviour and data

The **Corrected permission clock** column uses the producer's coherent snapshot
selector at midnight, rounded upwards to daily buckets, with a two-day age limit
against the selected clock. **Fixed framework** exercises the unchanged local
Trading Strategy client and actual executor. That client still uses the later
of price timestamp and `written_at`, ignoring `permission_observed_at`; it can
therefore disagree with the corrected result. The client must prefer the genuine
or inferred permission clock and retain clock quality and unknowns. A separate
[Trading Strategy issue #252](https://github.com/tradingstrategy-ai/trading-strategy/issues/252) describes that change; this analysis does not patch the
client or falsify the price write timestamps to make it appear fixed.

For **13 September–6 October**, the unchanged client now agrees with the corrected
midnight result because the restored rows retain their earlier write clocks.
Across the full calendar, it still reports Unknown where the corrected clock
reports Open on **6 March–6 April**. These 32 days precede the framework's
11 April historical cutoff, so both admission paths apply the existing assumed
Open policy there. The agreement in September does not remove the client clock
and freshness issue.
